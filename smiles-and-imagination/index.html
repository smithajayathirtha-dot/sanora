About us

Small by design, so every child is known.

We keep our group small and our days warm and unhurried. Children make friends, try new things and build confidence in a home setting that feels comfortable from the first day.

Our program

Everyday play that builds big skills

Our days are built around four things we nurture in every child.

Curiosity

Questions are welcome. Children explore, notice and discover the world around them.

Independence

Little routines, from washing hands to tidying up, help children feel capable.

Creativity

Art, music, stories and pretend play give every imagination room to run.

Confidence

Friendships and gentle encouragement help children speak up and try new things.
